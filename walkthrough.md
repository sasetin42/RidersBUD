# Service Detail View Enhancement Walkthrough

This document outlines how to test and verify the dynamic specifications and quotation-based notices implemented in `pages/ServiceDetailScreen.tsx`.

---

## 1. Dynamic Specifications Verification

To verify that the specifications cards render properly, navigate to or simulate different service types:

### A. Car Rental Service (e.g., `isCarRental === true`)
* **Expected UI**: Under the Title Card, a "Service Specifications" block will appear.
* **Fields Rendered**:
  * **Class**: Displays class (e.g., Sedan, SUV) with a Car icon.
  * **Transmission**: Displays transmission type (e.g., Automatic, Manual) with a Gear/Settings icon.
  * **Fuel Policy**: Displays fuel policy (e.g., Full to Full, Wet/Dry) with a Fuel icon.
* **Mock Verification**: Ensure the service record has `isCarRental: true`, and correct strings for `carRentalClass`, `carRentalTransmission`, and `carRentalFuel`.

### B. Driver Hire Service (e.g., `isDriverHire === true`)
* **Expected UI**: Under the Title Card, a "Service Specifications" block will appear.
* **Fields Rendered**:
  * **License**: Displays driver license requirement (e.g., Professional, Non-Pro) with a Shield/Document icon.
  * **Experience**: Displays driver experience (e.g., 5+ Years) with a User Check icon.
  * **Limits**: Displays geographic limits (e.g., Metro Manila) with a Map Pin icon.
* **Mock Verification**: Ensure the service record has `isDriverHire: true`, and correct strings for `driverLicenseType`, `driverExperience`, and `driverGeoLimits`.

---

## 2. Quotation Service Verification (e.g., `price <= 0`)

To verify quotation-based behavior:
1. Select a service with its price set to `0` or less.
2. Verify that:
   * The price in the title card displays a yellow/warning **"Quotation Required"** badge instead of `₱0`.
   * An orange alert notice block is rendered inside the **"About Service"** area stating: *"This service requires a custom quotation. The final pricing depends on your requirements and will be determined upon booking request approval."*
   * The primary booking button in the fixed footer changes its label from **"Book Now"** to **"Request Quotation"**.

---

## 3. Dynamic "About Service" Description Verification

1. View the **About Service** section for different service names.
2. Confirm the description has custom-tailored suffixes based on the service name:
   * **Towing/Roadside/Wrecker**: Shows specific roadside safety/towing assurance copy.
   * **Driver Hire/Chauffeur**: Shows vetted/safe navigation driver hire copy.
   * **Car Rental**: Shows fleet rental options copy.
   * **Brake/Mechanic/Repair**: Shows quality diagnostics and restoration copy.
   * **Other Services**: Appends standard tailor-made greeting based on the service name.

---
