# Mechanic Portal Enhancements: My Jobs & My Earnings

This update brings a significant visual and functional overhaul to the Mechanic's "My Jobs" and "My Earnings" screens, aligning them with the premium, glassmorphic design established in the Dashboard.

## Key Enhancements

### 1. "My Jobs" Screen
*   **Premium Design**: Replaced basic list items with modern, glassmorphic cards (`bg-[#1A1A1A]`) featuring subtle gradients and improved typography.
*   **Performance Snapshot**: Added a top section displaying real-time KPIs:
    *   **Active Tasks**: Count of currently active jobs.
    *   **Total Jobs**: All-time job count.
    *   **Completion Rate**: Percentage of successfully completed jobs.
    *   **"Live" Indicator**: A pulsing green dot to emphasize real-time data.
*   **Enhanced Functionality**:
    *   **Tabbed Navigation**: Easily switch between 'Active', 'Completed', and 'Cancelled' jobs.
    *   **Search**: Added a search bar to filter jobs by customer name, vehicle, or service.

### 2. "My Earnings" Screen
*   **Premium Visuals**: Updated the financial dashboard with the new dark theme and glassmorphic elements.
*   **Financial Snapshot**:
    *   **Total Earned**: Total earnings for the selected period.
    *   **Jobs Done**: Count of completed jobs in the period.
    *   **Avg. Job Value**: Average earning per job.
*   **Improved Information Architecture**:
    *   **Interactive Charts**: The bar chart has been visually upgraded with better gradients and rounded bars.
    *   **Transaction History**: A cleaner, grouped list of past earnings with clear "Paid" vs "Pending" status indicators.
    *   **Date Filters**: Toggle between "This Week", "This Month", and "All Time" to view relevant data.

These changes cumulatively provide a more professional, app-like experience for mechanics, giving them better visibility into their work and finances.
